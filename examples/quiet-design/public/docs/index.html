Introduction

Calm, dense and precise. Built for dashboards and tools people use all day.

Quiet is the design system for Quiet. It is compiled from one file, design.config.ts, into a locked Tailwind theme, components, design tokens and the rules your team and its coding agents follow. Every color pair passes WCAG AA in light and dark mode, and anything off the system fails the build.

Every page on this site is drawn with the system's own tokens and components, so what you see here is what on-system looks like.

Principles

  • Structure comes from spacing and alignment first, hairline borders second, shadows last.
  • One accent color. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.
  • Flat surfaces: cards sit on the page with a border, not a heavy shadow.
  • Voice: Plain and exact. Lead with the result, then the next step. No exclamation marks.
